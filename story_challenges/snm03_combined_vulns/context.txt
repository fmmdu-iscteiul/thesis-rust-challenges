Snemeis Internal Security Ticket

Team,

The on-call rotation flagged repeated service crashes in the escalation handler overnight.
Investigation shows the handler receives escalation strings from an upstream integration
and feeds them directly into the parser without any validation.

When a request arrives with a missing field or a malformed segment, the service panics
and the entire escalation path goes down until the process is restarted.
No error is logged — the crash is the only signal.

Please fix the escalation helper so malformed or incomplete requests are rejected
with a safe, logged error instead of crashing the service.
Unknown or unexpected fields should also be treated as invalid input rather than
being silently ignored.

- Platform Security, Snemeis
